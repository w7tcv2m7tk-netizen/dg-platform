## Objection handling

**"We already have HubSpot."**  
→ "Keep it if it's working. Most clients come to us when they're paying for Marketing Hub plus three other tools and data still doesn't connect. We replace the *stack*, not just CRM. Show the $348 vs $1,850 comparison."

**"Is this just WordPress plugins?"**  
→ "It's a modular business OS hosted on your WordPress site — you own the data, no per-contact HubSpot tax, and industry modules are built in. Client portal, Stripe provisioning, and onboarding run automatically on purchase."

**"What about onboarding / setup?"**  
→ "Stripe checkout creates the client user, sends onboarding link, and tags CRM. We still manually enable modules on their deployed site — factor 2–3 days in onboarding SLA. Training add-on $497 one-time."

**"Industry app for Finance/Services/Auto?"**  
→ "Pipeline CRM is live for founding customers at +$99/mo. Full quoting/invoicing is on the roadmap — set expectations clearly."

**"How does this compare to GoHighLevel?"**  
→ "Similar all-in-one ambition, but we're vertical-first (RE, STR, agency) with Oxygen-native frontends and Australian hosting. Better for businesses that want their own brand on their own domain."
