Commercial Model

Founding commercial model — access, not a % discount

Published pricing

Founding customers pay standard published Platform + Apps pricing. Try DigitalGate free for 14 days after you accept an offer and complete setup. Choose monthly or annual billing — annual ≈ 10 months of monthly pricing (save ~2 months).

Programme benefits

Limited places, priority onboarding, early access to selected Apps, founder relationship and roadmap influence. Founding 10 is limited access — not discounted access.

What is not included

Founding benefits do not automatically include Professional Services (including website builds), Customer Success / Support plans, custom development, consulting, migration or implementation — unless expressly included in your Founding offer.

Founding Referral Status

Direct referral rates by cohort: Founding 10 — 20% · Founding 100 — 15% · Founding 1,000+ — 10%. The separate Acquisition Partner programme (invitation only, 25%) is not the same. Referral status does not authorise you to bind DigitalGate, set pricing or represent yourself as an authorised agent unless separately agreed in writing.
