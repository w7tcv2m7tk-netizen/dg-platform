Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What's the difference between Platform and Growth Services?
The Platform is our SaaS software — you get full access to the CRM, automation, AI, and all features. Growth Services are managed services where our team executes strategy for you. You can buy one, the other, or both.
Can I use the Platform without Growth Services?
Yes. The Platform is a standalone product. You get full access to all software features. Growth Services are optional add-ons for businesses that want expert execution.
What's included in the Industry Apps?
Each Industry App adds specialised functionality for that vertical. Real Estate covers vendor leads and listings; Accommodation handles bookings and guests; Finance, Services, Automotive, and Commercial each include industry-specific pipelines and workflows.
What is Social Pro?
Social Pro lets you compose, schedule, and publish posts to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, and Pinterest from inside DG Platform — no switching between apps. Connect your accounts once, write once, and publish everywhere. Add it as a Premium App (+$79/mo) on top of any platform plan.
Are there lock-in contracts?
No. All plans are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.
What is the Founding Customer Program?
We are onboarding a limited number of pilot agencies at a discounted rate while we validate workflows and gather case studies. Roe Realty is our first live deployment. Founding customers get priority support and input into the product roadmap.
Do I need an Industry App?
Industry Apps are optional add-ons for businesses in specific verticals. The core platform works standalone. Add Real Estate, Finance, Accommodation, Services, Automotive, or Commercial when you need specialised pipelines and workflows.
How does pricing compare to HubSpot or Odoo?
Most businesses stack CRM, automation, SEO, scheduling, and forms across multiple tools costing $300–1,000+/month. DigitalGate consolidates these into one platform from $99/month, with industry apps and growth services as optional add-ons.
