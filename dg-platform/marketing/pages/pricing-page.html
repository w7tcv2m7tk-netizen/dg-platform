Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What's the difference between Platform, Apps, Services and Success?
Platform is the operating system (Starter / Growth / Scale / Enterprise). Apps are add-ons in Core → Infrastructure → Industry → Growth order. Professional Services are optional people (implementation, migration, training). Customer Success is ongoing support (Standard included; Priority and Success Partner optional). AI is built into the intelligence layer by platform tier. Growth Apps are optional and billed separately: Prospecting & Opportunity Engine $99 · AI Visibility $99 · SEO $99 · Automation $49 · Analytics $49 · Social $79 · AI Communications $99 · Reputation Free. Prospecting & Opportunity Engine is one App (Prospecting, Discovery and scoring included — not separate SKUs).
Do I need Professional Services or Customer Success?
No. The platform is self-service from day one with Standard support included. Add Professional Services for setup, migration or custom work. Add Priority or Success Partner if you want dedicated success management and strategy sessions.
What is the App hierarchy?
Canonical order is Core (run) → Infrastructure (power) → Industry (specialise) → Growth (grow). Core is included with the platform. Infrastructure is priced progressively as commercially ready. Industry and Growth Apps are optional add-ons billed separately — Growth: Prospecting & Opportunity Engine $99 · AI Visibility $99 · SEO $99 · Automation $49 · Analytics $49 · Social $79 · AI Communications $99 · Reputation Free. AI is an intelligence layer included by tier; specialist AI Apps are licensed as Growth Apps where applicable.
Is Website Builder fully included?
No. Platform Core includes website connection & management. Website Builder is software capability — create and manage websites in DigitalGate, progressively by platform tier. It is not a done-for-you agency build.
Website Migration vs Website Build — which do I need?
Website Migration & DigitalGate Setup (From $1,497) moves your existing website into DigitalGate Infrastructure so it runs and is managed on the platform (files, DNS, hosting, SSL, forms, redirects, testing and launch). It does not include redesign or redevelopment. Website Build (From $1,997) creates a new professional website on DigitalGate. After either path, you manage ongoing changes via Website Builder (by tier) or optional Professional Services / Success / Growth Apps.
What is a Website Build?
An optional Professional Service — a new website designed, configured and launched on DigitalGate. Public pricing is From $1,997 one-time; project price depends on scope, pages, integrations and content. Separate from Platform subscription and from Website Builder. Founding discounts on Platform + Apps do not automatically apply unless expressly included in your Founding offer.
What is Success Partner?
Success Partner ($499/mo) is a Customer Success plan — not marketing execution. You get a dedicated success manager, monthly strategy sessions, AI optimisation reviews, team training and quarterly roadmap planning.
Are there lock-in contracts?
No. All plans are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.
What is the Founding Customer Programme?
The early commercial path for businesses operating on DigitalGate. Founding 10 is open now — 30% off qualifying Platform + Industry Apps + additional Templates at onboarding for 24 months, plus referral tier benefits. Your own founding discount and referral commission are completely separate. Apps added after onboarding are at published pricing. Full rules: Founding Customer Terms.
Do I need an Industry App?
No. Industry Apps are optional. The core platform works standalone. Add an Industry App (Property, Services, Hospitality & Accommodation, …) when you need specialised workflows — and only when that Industry is ready for your stage (Available, Early Access or Coming Soon).
How do Industry Apps and Templates price?
Industry App = $99/mo — the major vertical capability. 1 Industry Template is included (your primary business model). Additional Templates = +$29/mo each. Example: Property with Real Estate = $99; add Property Management = $128. Templates are not separate Industry products.
Does the Founding discount cover Templates?
Yes — for Founding 10, the 30% acquisition discount applies to qualifying Platform + Industry Apps + any additional Industry Templates purchased as part of the Founding subscription at initial onboarding (24 months). Items added later are at published pricing. Full rules: Founding Customer Terms.
