Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What's the difference between Platform, Apps, and Services?
The Platform is the core software — CRM, AI, automation, and your Digital Twin. Apps add industry verticals and advanced capabilities like SEO or AI Visibility. Professional Services and Support Plans are optional — for implementation, training, and ongoing customer success.
Do I need Professional Services or a Support Plan?
No. The platform is self-service from day one with Standard support included. Add Professional Services for setup, migration, or custom work. Add a Support Plan if you want dedicated success management, training, and strategy sessions.
What's included in the Industry Apps?
Each Industry App adds specialised functionality for that vertical. Real Estate covers vendor leads and listings; Accommodation handles bookings and guests; Finance, Services, Automotive, and Commercial each include industry-specific pipelines and workflows.
Is my website included or a separate service?
Website management is a platform capability — not a separate agency retainer. Growth tier and above includes website tools, templates, and AI content. Advanced SEO and AI Visibility unlock as Apps. Need help migrating or building? That's Professional Services.
What is Success Partner?
Success Partner ($499/mo) is a customer success plan — not marketing execution. You get a dedicated success manager, monthly strategy sessions, AI optimisation reviews, team training, and quarterly roadmap planning to help you get maximum value from the platform.
What is the Social App?
Social lets you compose, schedule, and publish posts to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, and Pinterest from inside the platform. Add it as a Growth App (+$79/mo) on top of any platform plan.
Are there lock-in contracts?
No. All plans are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.
What is the Founding Customer Program?
We are onboarding a limited number of pilot agencies at a discounted rate while we validate workflows and gather case studies. Roe Realty is our first live deployment. Founding customers get priority support and input into the product roadmap.
Do I need an Industry App?
Industry Apps are optional add-ons for businesses in specific verticals. The core platform works standalone. Add Real Estate, Finance, Accommodation, Services, Automotive, or Commercial when you need specialised pipelines and workflows.
How does pricing compare to stacked SaaS tools?
Most businesses stack CRM, automation, SEO, scheduling, and forms across multiple tools costing $300–1,000+/month. DigitalGate consolidates these categories into one platform from $99/month, with industry and growth Apps as optional add-ons.
