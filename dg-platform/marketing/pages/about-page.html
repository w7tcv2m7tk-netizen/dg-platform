About DigitalGate

The Gateway to Your Digital World™

Connecting Businesses. Centralising Data. Powering Growth.

DigitalGate is an AI-powered Business Operating Platform designed to connect, centralise and intelligently manage every aspect of a business's digital world.

Rather than replacing the software businesses already use, DigitalGate becomes the intelligent layer that connects websites, CRM, AI, marketing, automation, communications, payments, analytics and industry-specific systems into one unified platform.

One login. One dashboard. One source of truth.

Whether you're a real estate agency, accommodation provider, finance business, professional service or growing enterprise, DigitalGate helps simplify operations, automate workflows, improve visibility and unlock better business decisions through connected data and artificial intelligence.

Meet the Founder

Meet Ben Roe

Ben Roe

Founder, Software Architect & Business Strategist

DigitalGate was founded by Ben Roe, an entrepreneur with more than two decades of experience spanning real estate, commercial property, digital marketing, automation and business technology.

Ben's career began in commercial property in South West London before continuing across Sydney and South East Queensland, working within commercial property, residential sales, business development, property management and agency operations.

Over the years, one recurring problem became increasingly obvious.

Businesses were investing in more technology than ever before, yet everything remained disconnected.

Customer data lived in one system. Marketing lived somewhere else. Websites, automation, payments, AI tools, reporting and communications all operated independently.

Instead of making businesses more efficient, technology was creating more complexity.

DigitalGate was created to solve that problem.
